Attendance App Guide

Master Your Attendance

The complete student's guide to tracking your progress, analyzing your performance, and staying on top of your goals.

Step 1

A Seamless Login

  • Creating an Account: If you're logging in for the first time, simply enter your UUCMS ID and create a new password to sign up.
  • Quick Login: Already have an account? Just enter your UUCMS ID and existing password.
  • Zero Hassle: Simple, intuitive setup means you get access in seconds.
Step 2

The Daily Tab

  • Real-time Status: View your attendance accurately for today.
  • Peace of Mind: Keep track of your daily schedule effortlessly.
  • Instant Alerts: Receive live notifications for important daily events.
  • Daily Overview: A quick glance gives you the entire day's progress.
Step 3

Overall Attendance

  • Subject Breakdown: Quickly see exactly what percentage of classes you've attended for each individual subject.
  • Clear Percentages: Straightforward numbers showing exactly where you stand in every course.
  • Track Requirements: Easily check if you are meeting the minimum attendance criteria for your classes.
  • Simple & Direct: Get the exact information you need at a glance without unnecessary clutter.
Step 4

Performance Insights

  • Visual Graphs: See your attendance history mapped out clearly on an interactive graph.
  • 75% Target Tracker: Instantly know exactly how many more classes you need to attend to safely cross the 75% minimum.
  • Attendance Patterns: Easily spot trends and maintain your streak over the semester.
  • Stay on Track: Keep yourself motivated and ensure you never fall behind on requirements.
Step 5

Profile Management

  • Self-Service: Update your contact info securely at any time.
  • Custom Notifications: Preferences ensure you only get the updates you want.
  • Easy Support: Immediate access to help and support whenever needed.
  • Independence: Manage your profile entirely on your own.
